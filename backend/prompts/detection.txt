## Your Job
Analyze the telemetry snapshot below and determine:
1. The incident severity (CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW)
2. Which service is affected
3. A clear, one-sentence human-readable summary for the incident title
4. A short paragraph summary explaining what anomaly was detected and why it's significant

## Telemetry Snapshot
Service: {service}
Health Status: {health_status}
HTTP Status Code: {http_status_code}

## Rules
- If health_status is "unhealthy" or http_status_code >= 500, this is at minimum HIGH severity
- "connection pool exhausted" or "ECONNREFUSED" patterns indicate CRITICAL database issues
- Be factual and precise — do not speculate beyond the data provided

Respond with ONLY a valid JSON object matching the required schema.
